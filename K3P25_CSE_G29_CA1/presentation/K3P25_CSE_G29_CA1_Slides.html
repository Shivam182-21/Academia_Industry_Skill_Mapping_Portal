01 | Introduction

The problem, our idea, and what our portal is designed to do.

02

THE PROBLEM

Academic syllabi update on 2–3 year cycles, while tech recruiters demand modern tools (FastAPI, React, Docker). Students lack visibility into missing skills, and CGPA alone fails to reflect production competency.

OUR SOLUTION

A centralized web platform where course syllabi and recruiter job descriptions are ingested to extract skills, evaluate gaps using Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive depths, and deliver transparent placement telemetry.

OUR GOAL

Make skill discovery easier, eliminate placement guesswork, automate curriculum telemetry for faculty, and provide transparent candidate shortlisting for recruiters.

02 | Dataset & Data

What information the application stores and how it is used.

03

DATA SOURCE

Our portal uses authentic academic and recruitment documents:

  • Course outlines from LPU (Data Structures, DBMS, Cloud DevOps)
  • Verified corporate job postings (TechCorp, CloudScale, Ayush)
  • 30 verified ground-truth skills across 6 test documents
  • Required Bloom's cognitive depth ratings (Levels 1–6)

Reports are stored in an SQLite / PostgreSQL database.

HOW DATA SUPPORTS MATCHING

  • Text fields are parsed with boundary-aware regex heuristics (\b...\b).
  • Common aliases (e.g. 'Postgres', 'pgSQL') map to canonical taxonomy slugs.
  • Bloom's cognitive depth scales skill importance (Level 1–2: 1.0x, Level 3–4: 1.5x, Level 5–6: 2.0x).
  • Certified student evidence validates demonstrated proficiency (c_i ∈ {0.0, 0.5, 1.0}).

Important: Evaluated on ground truth with zero data fabrication.

READINESS SCORING USED IN THE CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

Unweighted Coverage = (|S_student ∩ S_job| / |S_job|) × 100%  •  Weighted Readiness = [ ∑(w_i · c_i · α(b_i)) / ∑(w_i · α(b_i)) ] × 100%
Cognitive Multipliers: Bloom Level 1–2: 1.0x (Recall) • Level 3–4: 1.5x (Apply/Analyze) • Level 5–6: 2.0x (Architect/Deploy)

04 | Research Foundation

Research papers behind the NLP extraction and cognitive weighting used in our portal.

05

PAPER 1 | COMPETENCY TAXONOMY & ONTOLOGY

Zhang, L., Wang, X., & Liu, Y. (2021).
“Ontology-based competency modeling and curriculum mapping in computing education.”
IEEE Transactions on Education.

Relevance to our Portal: Zhang et al. explored formal ontology mapping for CS curricula. Our system extends this with dynamic regex boundary rules and fast alias lookup tables that support rapidly changing developer libraries.

PAPER 2 | COGNITIVE DEPTH IN LEARNING OUTCOMES

Anderson, L. W. & Krathwohl, D. R. (2001).
“A Taxonomy for Learning, Teaching, and Assessing: A Revision of Bloom's Taxonomy.”
New York: Longman.

Relevance to our Portal: Bloom's revised taxonomy provides the cognitive scaling framework for our gap engine. We assign higher weights to synthesis and evaluation skills (Levels 5–6) compared to basic recall (Levels 1–2).

RESEARCH GAP / PROJECT MOTIVATION

Existing resume matchers act as unexplainable black boxes or simple keyword counts. Our portal combines deterministic regex extraction, Bloom's cognitive depth weighting, and explainable multi-stakeholder dashboards in a single campus placement system.

06 | EVALUATION

Preliminary Matching Evaluation

How our portal scores, compares against the baseline, and ranks student placement readiness.

09

WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES

For each target job role, our portal compares required competencies with student acquired skills and calculates both Unweighted Coverage % and Weighted Placement Readiness Index.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Unweighted coverage indicates simple skill presence. Weighted readiness evaluates cognitive depth and employer criticality—a student missing Docker or SQL indexing cannot be ranked production-ready.

READINESS SIGNALS & EMPIRICAL RESULTS

MULTI-SIGNAL PIPELINE: Syllabus Text • Regex Boundary (\b...\b) • Synonym Map • Bloom Depth • Verified Evidence
